# Surface: the Keyflow app shell (setup and play screen)

Mode: Operate. The learner sets up once per session, then plays with hands on the keys and reads the screen from a stand's distance in a dim room. Success: a first visit makes the next step obvious, and the keyboard and sound are always one step away. Must stay: every behaviour, test id, label and native control; no layout shift in the Sound status before audio runs; canvas draws only after a change. Wrong if: childish, pro-gear, or generic SaaS (PRODUCT.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Settings are a crate of divider cards and sounds are sleeves you pull from it; refuses the category's stacked grey settings form with one accent.
OWN-WORLD: Walnut-black ground (#1c1512), flat #2a201a cards with kraft (#c8a271) tabs lettered in Big Shoulders Display capitals; each sound owns one colour everywhere (Funk Lead #ff7b2e, Wobble Bass #ff7fae, EP Keys #7cc4f2); yellow #ffd25a means next step, green #7fd49b done; round stickers, pill buttons; no red, no shadows but the lifted sleeve's floor.
