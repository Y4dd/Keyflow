# P2-T7 manual check: progress view

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off. Do a few drills first (Play the chord, about six answers) so the Progress card has something in it. If the card stays empty (drills record attempts only once P2-T4 is in), judge the looks from the legend row, which shows each mark. About 3 minutes, all taste: nothing waits on it.

What changed: a Progress card above "Your data" shows a grid of the 12 keys across, chord and scale types down, each square marking where that item stands (not open yet, new, shaky, known cold), and a row of dots for the last 14 days with one line about how often you practised. Recording and the counts are tested automatically.

### 1. Pick the look of the grid (taste)
Step back to where you play from. Switch the **Fill**, **Stickers** and **Tint** chips over the grid and look at each from there.
- **Pass:** you name the one you'd keep (`P2-T7 taste: stickers`). In each, known cold should stand out, shaky should read as "partly there", and the not-open squares should stay quiet.
- **Fail:** in all three you can't tell shaky from known cold, or new from not open, at that distance. Say which pair blurs.

### 2. The practice days feel gentle (taste)
Read the line and the dots above the legend.
- **Pass:** it reads as an encouraging record of the days you played, and an empty dot for a missed day doesn't feel like a mark against you.
- **Fail:** it feels like a streak you can lose or a telling-off. Say what you'd change (drop the "days in a row", fewer days, no dots).
