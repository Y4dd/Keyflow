# P1-T8 manual check: take stats

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones, the Timing card showing a calibration (run it first if it says none). Start audio and keep the Electro-funk vamp in D minor with Count-in on. About 4 minutes.

What changed: under a take's piano roll the Takes card now shows its notes, range, how many notes were in the scale, how many of the notes on beats 1 and 3 were chord tones, the longest rest, and one suggestion. The counting and the rules are tested automatically; these steps check that the beat-1-and-3 count follows your real playing, through the keyboard's real timing.

### 1. Notes on the beat count as on it (must-pass)
Press **Record**, and after the count-in play only **D** (any octave), once on beat 1 and once on beat 3 of every bar, for 8 bars (16 notes). Press **Save take**.
- **Pass:** Notes reads 16 (or the number you played) and Chord tones on 1 and 3 reads 100% with all or nearly all of them counted (say "16 of 16"; one or two missing is fine).
- **Fail:** many fewer are counted than you played (e.g. "9 of 9" when you played 16: your on-beat notes are judged off the beat; say whether you think you were early or late), or it reads well under 100%.

### 2. The chord changes are followed (must-pass)
Record again, this time only **C**, on beats 1 and 3 for 8 bars. C is in Dm7 but not in G7.
- **Pass:** Chord tones on 1 and 3 reads about 50% (C counts over the two Dm7 bars and not over the two G7 bars).
- **Fail:** it reads 100% or 0%, or far from half.

### 3. The suggestion's tone (taste)
Record a minute of free playing and read the stats and the one suggestion under the roll.
- **Pass:** the suggestion reads like a friendly tip you might try, not a grade, and the numbers are easy to read from the stand. Or say what you'd change (a rule, the wording, a stat to drop).
- **Fail:** it reads like a report card or a telling-off, or the numbers are too small to read from where you play.
