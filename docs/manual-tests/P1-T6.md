# P1-T6 manual check: the mixer

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio and keep the default split (Round Bass in the left hand, Funk Lead in the right). About 5 minutes.

What changed: the Groove card has a **Mix** section with a level slider and a Mute box for Drums, Bass, Chords and Your synth, plus **You play bass**. That each control sets the right level, is saved, and that "you play bass" silences the groove's bass while the split is on are tested automatically; these steps are only about how it sounds.

### 1. Levels and mutes while a groove plays (must-pass)
Press Play on the Electro-funk vamp. Drag each slider down and back up, then tick and untick each Mute box, while the groove plays and while you hold a note.
- **Pass:** each control changes only its own part (Your synth changes only what you play), smoothly, with no click or pop when a part mutes or comes back.
- **Fail:** a click or pop on mute or unmute, a slider moving the wrong part, or a part that stays silent after unmuting (say which).

### 2. You play bass (must-pass)
Tick **You play bass**, play a bass line in your left hand over the groove, then untick it. Then turn the split off (Play card) with it ticked.
- **Pass:** ticked, the groove's bass is gone and your left-hand bass is the only bass, drums and chords unchanged; unticked, the groove's bass is back. With the split off, the groove's bass plays even while ticked.
- **Fail:** the groove's bass keeps playing under yours, the drums or chords drop out too, or it clicks as the bass mutes.

### 3. Count-in with the drums muted (must-pass)
Stop the groove, mute **Drums**, keep Count-in on, and press Play.
- **Pass:** you still hear the one bar of count-in clicks, then the groove with no drums.
- **Fail:** the count-in is silent, or the drums play.

### 4. Default balance (taste)
Unmute everything, set every slider back to 0 dB, and solo over the groove for a minute.
- **Pass:** your synth sits clearly over the groove at the defaults, or you name the level you'd rather start at (say "synth +3" or "drums −3").
- **Fail:** the defaults are so far off that every session starts with moving sliders (say which way).
