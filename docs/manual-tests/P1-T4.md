# P1-T4 manual check: highlighting and training wheels

**Setup:** Chrome on the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/), the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones, sitting at the keyboard as you would to practise. Start audio and keep Split on (Wobble Bass left, Funk Lead main). About 10 minutes.

What changed: the canvas keyboard now shows the groove's harmony. **Big dots** (in the colour of the sound that key plays) mark the current chord's tones, **small kraft dots** the rest of the solo scale, and the other keys are **dimmed grey**. With training wheels on **Names** the marked keys also show their note names. While a groove plays the dots move to the next chord half a beat before it sounds. The Groove card has a **Scale** menu and **Training wheels** chips (Names, Dots, Chord tones, Off). Which keys get which mark, the scale menu, and that the dots move on time are all tested automatically. This check is about your hands, ears and eyes.

If you also run P1-T0 step 1, note that the dots and grey keys are this highlighting. They aren't lit keys: a lit key is filled with its sound's colour.

### 1. Playing stays instant while the dots follow the chords (must-pass)
Pick the Electro-funk vamp, press Play and solo with your right hand for a minute or two, through several i7 → IV7 changes.
- **Pass:** playing feels as instant as before, and the groove doesn't stumble when the dots move to the next chord.
- **Fail:** lag that wasn't there before, or a hiccup or flam in the drums or bass at each chord change. Say which.

### 2. Readable from where you sit (taste)
Glance at the keys from your playing position while the groove runs.
- **Pass:** you can tell a big dot from a small one at a glance. The small dots show on the white keys. The grey keys read as "not now", without looking broken or like black keys. The note names and the computer-key letters under them don't get mixed up.
- **Answer:** `fine`, or what to change (for example "bigger dots", "less grey", "names too small").

### 3. The chord change shows half a beat early (taste)
Watch the IV7 dots appear just before the chord changes, and try landing on one (the B is new there).
- **Pass:** the early warning helps you aim for the next chord.
- **Answer:** `keep`, `earlier` (a whole beat), or `on the beat`.

### 4. Which training-wheels level opens first (taste)
The app starts on **Names**. Try **Dots** and **Chord tones** for a minute each.
- **Answer:** `keep Names`, or the level that should open first.
