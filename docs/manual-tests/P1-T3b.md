# P1-T3b manual check: Warm Tines, the warm electric piano

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio, keep Split on (Round Bass left), pick **Warm Tines** as the main sound. About 10 minutes.

What changed: **Warm Tines** is a new main-sound sleeve (teal): a mellow electric piano with a slow tremolo, original and not modelled on a recording you can check against, so only your ears can say whether it's the sound you want. That it plays clean, stays below clipping and stops after release is tested automatically. Nothing waits on these steps: they're all **taste**.

### 1. The sound (taste)
Play slow chords and a few single notes, soft and hard, with no groove running.
- **Pass:** warm, round and mellow; playing hard adds a little bite (bark) without turning glassy.
- **Fail:** too bright, too dull, too much or too little bark, too short or too long, or simply not the keys sound you're after (say what's off: "brighter", "longer", "more bark").
- Then try the **Tone** slider (1.2 kHz to start): lower is warmer and darker, higher is brighter. If you'd move it, name the value you like.

### 2. The tremolo (taste)
Hold a chord and move the **Tremolo** slider (it starts at 30%).
- **Pass:** at the default the level swells gently; you can turn it up for more wash or down to none.
- **Fail:** the default is too much or too little, or the speed feels wrong (name the value you'd like, or "faster" or "slower").

### 3. In the grooves (taste)
Press Play on the Electro-funk vamp and the Soul groove and comp chords with Warm Tines over them. The grooves' own chords still play EP Keys.
- **Answer:** keep EP Keys for the grooves' chords, or switch them to Warm Tines.

Reply like `P1-T3b taste: 1 good, tone 900 Hz, 2 tremolo 20%, 3 keep EP Keys`.
