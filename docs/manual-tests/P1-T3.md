# P1-T3 manual check: are the four grooves fun to play over?

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio, keep Split on (Round Bass left, Funk Lead main). About 15 minutes.

**Re-run with the new bass (P1-T3b):** the first run couldn't rate the grooves because their Wobble Bass sounded harsh. They now play **Round Bass**, a clean, warm bass, under the EP Keys chords, so judge the grooves as they sound now.

What changed: the Groove card has a **Groove** menu with four original grooves; picking one sets its own key and tempo, and while playing it restarts from the top. That each groove plays, stays in key, keeps a playable register in all 12 keys and leaves no note sounding is tested automatically; only you can say whether it's fun. For each groove, press Play and solo with your right hand for a couple of minutes, using the notes of the scale named below.

All steps are **taste**: a "not fun" answer becomes a change to that groove, and nothing waits on it.

### 1. Electro-funk vamp: D minor, 108 BPM, i7–IV7 (taste)
Solo with D minor pentatonic (D F G A C), then add B for the Dorian sound.
- **Pass:** you want to keep playing; the syncopated bass and the stabs leave room for the lead.
- **Fail:** dull, too busy, too fast or slow, or it reminds you of a specific song (name what's off).

### 2. Soul groove: E♭ major, 84 BPM, Imaj7–vi7–ii7–V7 (taste)
Solo with E♭ major pentatonic (E♭ F G B♭ C).
- **Pass:** laid back, the backbeat sits behind you, the four chords move nicely.
- **Fail:** feels stiff or rushed, the swing is too much or too little, the chords are too loud, or it sounds like a known song.

### 3. Slow jam: D♭ major, 70 BPM, IVmaj7–iii7–ii7–Imaj7 (taste)
Solo with D♭ major pentatonic (D♭ E♭ F A♭ B♭), slowly, leaving space.
- **Pass:** half-time and lush, the chords ring under long lead notes.
- **Fail:** too empty, the snare on beat 3 feels wrong, the chords are muddy, or it sounds like a known song.

### 4. Boogie: E minor, 116 BPM, i7–iv7 (taste)
Solo with E minor pentatonic (E G A B D), then try the blue note B♭.
- **Pass:** it drives; the four-on-the-floor kick and octave bass make you want to move.
- **Fail:** the octave bass is too busy or too thin, the claps or open hats grate, or it sounds like a known song.

### 5. Which groove opens first (taste)
The Electro-funk vamp opens when the app starts.
- **Answer:** keep it, or name the groove that should open first.

Reply like `P1-T3 taste: 1 fun, 2 swing too heavy, 3 fun, 4 bass too busy, 5 keep`.
