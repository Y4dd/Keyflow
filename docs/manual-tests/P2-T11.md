# P2-T11 manual check: how the roll marks a take

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome on the laptop, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, sound on. In Jam, pick the Electro-funk vamp in D minor pentatonic. About 5 minutes.

What changed: after a take, the piano roll in Jam's Takes drawer marks each note by what the stats count. Chord tones on beats 1 and 3 are brightest, scale tones are filled, and notes outside the scale are hollow and dim. Bar lines and beats 1 and 3 sit behind the notes, and the longest rest is shaded. The suggestion's evidence is drawn in dashed bone. Pointing at a stat (or tapping it, or Tab to it) lights only the notes it counts. The counts and the marks agreeing is tested automatically; only the look is yours to judge.

### 1. Bar lines and the rest band read (taste)
Press Record, play about 8 bars: some notes on beats 1 and 3, a few notes outside the scale (E♭, B), one long rest. Press Save take and open the Takes drawer.
- **Looks right:** under the chord strip, a line at each bar and a fainter one at beat 3, and a shaded band where you rested longest.
- **Taste:** say if the lines or the band are too faint or too loud, e.g. `P2-T11 taste: bar lines fainter`.

### 2. Pick the marking look (taste)
Under the roll, switch between **Fill**, **Dot**, **Ring** and **Kraft**, looking from where you sit at the keys:
- **Fill:** chord tones on 1 and 3 are brighter and a little taller.
- **Dot:** a dot over the head of each note on 1 or 3; filled for a chord tone, a ring for another note.
- **Ring:** chord tones on 1 and 3 have a thin outline around them.
- **Kraft:** chord tones on 1 and 3 are in the tabs' tan.

In all four, a note on 1 or 3 that isn't a chord tone gets a short tick at its start (Dot uses its ring for this instead), and notes outside the scale are hollow.
- **Answer:** the one that reads best from the stand, e.g. `P2-T11 taste: dot`.

### 3. A stat lights its notes (taste)
Point at **In the scale**, then **Chord tones on 1 and 3**, then **Longest rest**.
- **Pass:** the other notes fade, so the counted ones stand out; Longest rest brightens its band.
- **Taste:** say if the fade is too strong or too weak, e.g. `P2-T11 taste: fade weaker`.
