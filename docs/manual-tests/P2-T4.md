# P2-T4 manual check: hints and grading

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, the on-screen keyboard in view from where you play. Start audio; no groove needed. About 6 minutes.

What changed: the Drills card now gives hints in steps when you stall (a line under the prompt, and the keys it means marked on the on-screen keyboard), rates each answer (Again, Hard, Good, Easy), and saves it. The ratings, the hint delays and the steps are tested automatically; these steps check that the hints read well from the stand and come at a pace that helps. Three temporary chips under the card set the pace: **Hints: Quick** (first hint after 4 s for a new item), **Steady** (6 s) and **Patient** (10 s). The wait grows for items you know well.

### 1. Hint keys show on the keyboard (must-pass)
Pick **Play the chord** and don't play. Wait through all three hints (about 20 s at Steady).
- **Pass:** "Hint 1" marks one key (the root, with its name); "Hint 2" marks the chord built up from it, labelled R, +3, +4 (or similar); "Hint 3" marks the whole shape with note names. Each is easy to read from where you play. Playing it then asks "once more, on your own", and the marks go away.
- **Fail:** a hint line appears but no keys are marked, the marked keys don't match the line, or the labels are too small to read from the stand.

### 2. The hint pace (taste)
Answer about four drills at each of **Hints: Quick**, **Steady** and **Patient**, mixing **Play the chord**, **Chord in key** and **Find the note**. Take your time on some of them.
- **Pass:** name the pace where hints come when you're stuck, not while you're still working it out (for example `P2-T4 taste: Steady`). That becomes the default and the chips go.
- **Fail:** none of them feels right. Say whether hints should come sooner or later.

### 3. Helpful, not naggy (taste)
At your chosen pace, do a few of each drill type, including **Play the scale** at 70 BPM (play the run slowly and steadily, and once stop halfway). Read each hint you get, and the rating after each right answer.
- **Pass:** the hints read as nudges that help you work it out (the method before the answer), no hint pops up while a scale run is going well, and the ratings feel fair. Or say what you'd change (a hint's wording, a step to drop, a rating that felt harsh).
- **Fail:** the hints feel like they're nagging or giving the answer away too early, or one comes in the middle of a steady scale run.
