# P1-T0 manual check: the redesign (Crate Dig)

**Setup:** Chrome on the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/), the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones, sitting at the keyboard as you would to practise. About 5 minutes.

What changed: the look only (colours, type, layout, the canvas keyboard's colours). Nothing on the audio or input path changed, and the e2e tests cover the controls, so this check is your hands, eyes and ears.

### 1. Playing still feels instant, and the right keys light (must-pass)
Connect, start sound, and keep Split on (Wobble Bass left, Funk Lead main). Play a fast run with your right hand, then a few left-hand bass notes, then some EP Keys chords (pick EP Keys as the main sound).
- **Pass:** it feels as instant as before. Each key lights on screen while you hold it, in the colour of the sound playing it (orange for Funk Lead, pink for Wobble Bass, blue for EP Keys), lighter for soft notes and brighter for hard ones, and goes dark the moment you lift.
- **Fail:** lag that wasn't there before, a key lit that you didn't press, a key that stays lit after you lift, or the wrong colour for the zone.

### 2. Connecting the keyboard ticks its step (must-pass)
The step order itself is unit-tested and the sound step is e2e-tested; only a real keyboard can tick the first one. Unplug the YPT-380, reload, then plug it in (press Connect if asked).
- **Pass:** before, "Connect keyboard" at the top right and the Keyboard card are outlined in yellow; once it connects, the sticker becomes a green ✓ "Keyboard connected" and the yellow moves to "Turn sound on" (or to "Calibrate timing" if sound is already on).
- **Fail:** the sticker stays yellow while the card says "Keyboard connected", or nothing turns yellow next.

### 3. Readable from where you sit (taste)
From your playing position, glance at the page: the step stickers, the card names (KEYBOARD, PLAY, MAIN SOUND…), the held-notes readout under the keys.
- **Pass:** you can read them at a glance.
- **Answer:** `fine`, or say what's too small (for example "held notes bigger").

### 4. The look (taste)
Does it feel warm, a little playful and grown-up, like an evening in a record shop? Is anything childish, pro-gear or generic?
- **Answer:** `keep`, or name what to change: a colour, the tab font, the faded unchosen sleeves, the yellow outlines.
