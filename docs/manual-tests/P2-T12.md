# P2-T12 manual check: views fit the laptop screen

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) on the laptop, browser window maximised at your usual zoom, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off. Steps 1–3 in Chrome, step 4 in Firefox in a private window (so it counts as a first visit). About 5 minutes.

What changed: the page is now three views in a top bar (Jam · Practice · Learn) plus Setup behind the gear, with one keyboard pinned at the bottom of every view. Switching views, the keyboard staying the same element, the groove playing on, and the first-visit flow are tested automatically at 1280×720; only your real screen is checked here.

### 1. Jam fits (must-pass)
Open Jam. Without scrolling, look for the whole keyboard (C2 to C7, bottom edge included), the readout line over it, and the transport (Play, Record, Groove, Key, Tempo, Scale, Help on the keys).
- **Pass:** all of it is on screen together.
- **Fail:** any of it is cut off or needs scrolling. Say what is cut off.

### 2. Practice fits with a drill open (must-pass)
Open Practice, press Start today's session, and wait for a hint (about 6 seconds without playing).
- **Pass:** the prompt, the hint line and the whole keyboard with the hint marks are on screen together, without scrolling.
- **Fail:** the prompt or the keyboard is off screen, or you have to scroll to see both.

### 3. The sound drawer leaves the keys alone (must-pass)
In Jam, press the Main pill at the right of the readout line (or the orange strip over the keys), play a few notes, then Close.
- **Pass:** the sounds open over the top part of the screen; the keyboard doesn't move and still plays; Close puts it back.
- **Fail:** the keyboard jumps or goes off screen, or notes stop working while it's open.

### 4. First visit in Firefox (must-pass)
In the private Firefox window, open the page. Press Connect on the Keyboard screen and accept the add-on prompt, then Next.
- **Pass:** the Sound screen shows "Sound is on", or its Start audio turns sound on in one click; Start playing lands on Jam and the keys sound.
- **Fail:** sound never starts on the Sound screen, or the keyboard isn't heard in Jam. Say what the screen showed.
