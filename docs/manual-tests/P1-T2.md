# P1-T2 manual check: the groove player

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, then Firefox for step 1; the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio, keep Split on (Wobble Bass left, Funk Lead main). About 15 minutes.

What changed: a Groove card under the keyboard plays a placeholder groove (drums, Wobble Bass, EP Keys) on the audio clock, and the Wobble Bass's wobble can follow the tempo (Wobble sync, in its sound card). The event list, count-in, stop, loop-off end and the synced wobble rate are tested automatically; only your ears can judge timing and feel.

### 1. Tight for five minutes, in both browsers (must-pass)
Press Play and let it loop for 5 minutes, in Chrome and then in Firefox. Every so often tap along on a key, or just listen to the kick, hats and bass together.
- **Pass:** the drums, bass and chords stay locked together and on the same tempo the whole time; nothing stutters, speeds up or slows down.
- **Fail:** the parts drift apart, a hit lands late or doubles, the groove hiccups (say when, and in which browser), or it crackles.

### 2. Count-in, Stop and Play (must-pass)
With Count-in on, press Play; after a while press Stop in the middle of a chord, then Play again straight away a few times.
- **Pass:** four clicks (the first higher), then the groove from the top; Stop silences everything at once and leaves no bass or chord note hanging.
- **Fail:** no count-in, the groove starts mid-bar, or a note keeps sounding after Stop.

### 3. Playing over it, changing key and tempo (must-pass)
While it plays, solo with your right hand and play a few bass notes with your left. Change the Key menu twice and drag Tempo from one end to the other.
- **Pass:** your playing feels as instant as without the groove; the groove moves to the new key within a bar or so and follows the tempo smoothly, with no stuck or wrong notes.
- **Fail:** your notes lag or crackle with the groove running, a note sticks after a key change, or the groove stumbles when the tempo moves.

### 4. The wobble sits in time (must-pass)
With Wobble sync on 8ths (the default), hold a low key with your left hand while the groove plays, then try 16ths.
- **Pass:** the wobble pulses in time with the groove (two pulses a beat on 8ths, four on 16ths), lined up with the hats.
- **Fail:** the wobble drifts against the beat, or doesn't change with the tempo.

### 5. Wobble sync default (taste)
- **Answer:** keep `8ths`, or pick `16ths` or `Free` as the default.

### 6. The drum kit and the balance (taste)
The drums are synthesized and the levels fixed until the mixer (P1-T6).
- **Answer:** `fine`, or name what's off (for example "kick too quiet", "hats too harsh", "chords too loud under the lead").
