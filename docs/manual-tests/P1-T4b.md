# P1-T4b manual check: the Tags and Spotlight highlighting looks

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio, keep Split on, and in the Groove card pick the Soul groove. About 8 minutes.

What changed: the highlighting dots are gone. The Groove card has a **Look** choice: **Tags** (a dark tag holding the name on each chord tone, light tags on black keys) and **Spotlight** (no marks: chord tones brightest, scale tones mid, the rest dark). Both were reworked for contrast after your comparison. A checkbox under the keyboard hides the computer-key letters (try it if you like). That every key gets the right mark, held keys stay distinct, and the look and the letters are saved is tested automatically.

### 1. Playing stays instant (must-pass)
Press Play, choose **Tags** at **Names**, and solo for a minute with fast runs and repeated notes. Then do the same with **Spotlight**.
- **Pass:** every key you press lights at once and the sound is as immediate as before; the highlighting follows the chord changes without hitching.
- **Fail:** a lit key lags behind your finger, the sound stutters or clicks, or the keyboard drawing hitches at a chord change (say which look).

### 2. Tags contrast (taste)
Stay on Tags and glance at the keys from where you play, at **Names**, then **No names**, then **Chord tones**.
- **Pass:** the chord tones jump out at a glance and the names read without leaning in.
- **Fail:** something is still hard to see: say what ("dashes too faint", "names too small", "tags on the black keys too bright").

### 3. Spotlight contrast (taste)
The same with Spotlight.
- **Pass:** you can tell chord tones, scale tones and the other keys apart at a glance.
- **Fail:** two of the steps look alike, or the dark keys feel too dark to play on (say which).

### 4. Default look (taste)
Think about which of the two you'd want to see first on a new visit.
- **Pass:** you name one: Tags (now the default) or Spotlight.
- **Fail:** neither feels right as the first view (say why).
