# P1-T5 manual check: pedal bends to the next scale tone

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome, the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, the sustain pedal plugged in, wired headphones. Start audio, keep Funk Lead as the main sound, and in the Pedal & bend card choose **Bend** and set **Bend amount** to **To next scale tone**. About 6 minutes.

What changed: the bend amount has a new option. With it, the pedal (or the bend key) bends each note up to the next note of the scale the Groove card shows, so it's 1, 2 or 3 semitones depending on the note (pentatonic and blues scales have 3-semitone gaps). That each note lands on the right scale tone, in every scale and key, is tested automatically; these steps are only about how it sounds and feels.

### 1. Bends sound musical over a groove (must-pass)
Press Play on the Electro-funk vamp (D minor pentatonic). Solo for a minute, pressing the pedal to bend into notes and lifting it to come back, on several different notes.
- **Pass:** every bend lands on a note that fits the groove, with no clicks, jumps or stuck pitches, and lifting the pedal brings the note back cleanly.
- **Fail:** a bend lands on a note that sounds wrong, clicks, overshoots, or the note stays bent after the pedal comes up (say which note).

### 2. Bending while changing notes (must-pass)
Hold the pedal down and play a short legato run up and down, then let go.
- **Pass:** each note sounds bent to its own next scale tone, and the run stays smooth.
- **Fail:** a note jumps or warbles as it starts, or a note sounds bent by the wrong amount.

### 3. The 3-semitone bends (taste)
In D minor pentatonic, bend D (to F) and A (to C): those are 3-semitone bends. Compare them with bending F (to G).
- **Pass:** the wide bends feel expressive and usable.
- **Fail:** the wide bends feel too far or too slow; say whether you'd rather they stop at 2 semitones (landing between scale tones) or take a shorter Bend time.

### 4. Default bend amount (taste)
Think about whether To next scale tone should be the default bend amount instead of 2 semitones.
- **Pass:** you name one: 2 semitones (now the default) or To next scale tone.
- **Fail:** neither feels right (say why).
