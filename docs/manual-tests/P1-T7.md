# P1-T7 manual check: record, replay, export

**Setup:** the deployed page (https://y4dd.github.io/Keyflow/) in Chrome (step 3 again in Firefox), the YPT-380 plugged in with Local Control off, wired headphones. Start audio, keep the default split and the Electro-funk vamp with Count-in on. About 7 minutes.

What changed: the Groove card has a **Record** button beside Play, and a new **Takes** card lists your takes with a piano roll of the chosen one, **Replay** and **Export .mid**. That a take is saved, survives a reload, replays exactly the events recorded, releases every note, the pedal and a bend when stopped mid-replay, and exports the notes played is tested automatically (through the simulated keyboard); these steps are about your real playing and how it sounds.

### 1. Record a take (must-pass)
Press **Record**, wait for the count-in, play a short phrase for a few bars (some notes on the beat, one held note, the pedal once), then press **Save take**.
- **Pass:** the take appears at the top of the Takes card (Electro-funk vamp, D minor, its length, now), and the roll shows your notes in the order and rhythm you played them, starting where you started, with Dm7 and G7 along the top changing where you heard the chords change.
- **Fail:** no take appears, notes are missing or doubled, or the roll puts your notes clearly early or late against the chord changes (say which way, roughly how much).

### 2. Replay sits on the groove (must-pass)
Press **Replay** and listen through to the end without playing.
- **Pass:** the groove starts from the top and your phrase comes back on it as you played it: same notes, same feel against the drums, the held note held, nothing hanging when it ends by itself.
- **Fail:** your notes rush or drag against the groove, sound flammed or uneven, a note keeps sounding after the end, or the groove plays on after your take ends.

### 3. Replay timing in Firefox (must-pass)
Record a new short take in **Firefox** (step 1), then replay it as in step 2.
- **Pass:** it sits on the groove as well as in Chrome.
- **Fail:** the replay is clearly earlier or later than you played (Firefox's MIDI arrives about 25 ms late, which a replay could shift), or uneven.

### 4. The piano roll from the stand (taste)
Look at the roll from where you play.
- **Pass:** you can tell your phrases apart and see the chord changes; notes outside the scale read dimmer, not as mistakes. Or name what you'd change ("taller roll", "chord tones brighter").
- **Fail:** it's too small or too faint to read from the stand (say which).

### 5. Export (taste)
Press **Export .mid** and open the file in any program that plays MIDI files, if you have one (skip otherwise).
- **Pass:** your notes play back in time at the groove's tempo; the first bar is the count-in.
- **Fail:** the file won't open, or the notes are at the wrong tempo or out of order.
